The Model

Five moves from wearable to premium.

Every FitInsured account follows the same clean sequence. No paperwork between steps — data flows, the score updates, the rate adjusts.

01

Connect wearables

Link Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, Garmin, or Strava. One secure handshake and the data begins to flow.

02

Track health metrics

Steps, heart rate, sleep, and active minutes are recorded continuously and rendered as a clear, honest baseline.

03

Earn FitCoin

Movement converts into FitCoin — our reward unit. The more consistently you move, the more you accumulate.

04

AI risk scoring

Our model reads your metrics and produces a live, individualized risk score — updated as your habits change.

05

Lower insurance rates

A stronger score means a lower premium. The savings are the natural output of the structure — earned, not negotiated.
