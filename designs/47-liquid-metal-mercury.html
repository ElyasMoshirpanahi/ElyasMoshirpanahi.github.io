For insurers

Partner API — anonymized risk, on tap

Insurance companies plug into FitInsured to access privacy-preserving, AI-scored risk data through a single REST endpoint.

POST /v1/risk/score
# Anonymized cohort risk score
curl https://api.fitinsured.io/v1/risk/score \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  -d '{ "cohort":"active-30-45",
       "signals":["steps","hrv","sleep"] }'

# → response
{ "risk_index": 0.34,
  "confidence": 0.92,
  "pii": "none" }
  • Anonymized by defaultNo raw PII ever leaves the platform — aggregated, consented signals only.
  • REST + webhooksScore cohorts on demand or subscribe to live risk-index updates.
  • Actuarial-grade modelsThe same AI risk engine that reprices consumer policies.
