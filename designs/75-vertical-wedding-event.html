The Journey to Lower Rates

From the wearable on your wrist to the premium on your policy — five graceful steps that turn movement into savings.

  1. i. Connect Wearables

    Link Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, Garmin, or Strava in moments — your devices join the celebration.

  2. ii. Track Health

    Steps, heart rate, sleep, and activity are gathered gently and securely into your personal health story.

  3. iii. Earn FitCoin

    Every mile and every workout is rewarded with FitCoin — the currency of a life well moved.

  4. iv. AI Risk Score

    Our AI reads your activity to craft a personalized, ever-refining risk score — the more you move, the better it reads.

  5. v. Lower Premiums

    A healthier score unlocks lower insurance rates — savings you can toast to, month after month.
