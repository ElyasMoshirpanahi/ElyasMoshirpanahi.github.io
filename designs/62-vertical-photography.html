Anonymized risk, on demand

FitInsured's partner API gives insurance companies structured, privacy-preserving access to aggregated activity risk — no personal data, just the signal you underwrite on.

  • Anonymized by design. Cohort-level risk scores, never raw personal health records.
  • Real-time endpoints. Pull fresh AI risk bands as member activity evolves.
  • Underwriting-ready. Feed scores straight into pricing and eligibility models.
